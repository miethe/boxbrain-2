# Storefront M1: BoxBrain v2 carryover

The storefront is an entry to the existing BoxBrain vision, using the fictional Northstar practice. The M0 directions supply hierarchy; the v2 prototype supplies the visual language.

- **Tokens:** slate canvas and text, white surfaces, fine slate borders, dark `#0b1220` navigation, blue `#2563eb` primary, violet AI and green/amber status semantics; Instrument Sans/Inter system fallback, compact radii and restrained shadows.
- **Components:** sidebar navigation, clear page headings and search, bordered cards, compact metadata chips, library-style asset cards and the numbered Play Flow. The offering guide uses a prominent bordered surface. Links remain real links and the situation doorway works without JavaScript.
- **Seller:** Field Guide ordering puts search and plays first, then ready assets, the problem-first doorway and offerings. Play Flow and library cards borrow the v2 visual patterns without claiming prototype functionality.
- **Org-wide:** Editorial Atlas contributes the outcome-first story, entry cards and numbered offerings; the same v2 tokens and surfaces keep it related to the seller edition. Seller-only Plays are absent.
- **Later application scope:** Ask/AI, accounts, opportunities, storyboards and the work-product builder remain in the larger app vision. This storefront does not reproduce their controls or imply those flows exist.

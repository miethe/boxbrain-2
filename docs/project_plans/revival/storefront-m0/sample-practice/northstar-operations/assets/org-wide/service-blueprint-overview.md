# Service Blueprint overview
Fictional Markdown deck source; each section is one proposed slide.

## 1. Make the service visible
Map the journey, find the handoffs, agree what changes. Start with one service whose work crosses team boundaries.

## 2. How the work unfolds
Listen to teams, map the handoffs, then agree a shared blueprint. Include the people receiving and handing over the work.

## 3. What teams leave with
A shared service map and an improvement backlog with named owners. These are proposed deliverables, not guaranteed operational benefits.

## 4. A useful first conversation
Choose one service boundary, one sponsor and a small group of people who do the work. Agree access and scope before an engagement.

# Handoff workshop guide
Seller-only fictional Markdown deck source; each section is one proposed slide.

## 1. Agree the boundary
Name one request, its starting point and its completion point. Invite people who hand over and receive the work.

## 2. Trace a real example safely
Ask each team what it receives, does and passes on. Use sanitized descriptions; never place confidential client details in workshop materials without clearance.

## 3. Mark uncertainty
Identify waits, missing information and ownership disagreements. Do not assign blame or infer causation from one example.

## 4. Choose a next step
Agree one question to investigate and who owns it. Confirm a follow-up conversation rather than a promised benefit.

# Decision Flow questions
Seller-only one-page conversation aid. Fictional sample.

- Which recent decision waited longer than expected?
- What information was required before an answer was possible?
- Who could say yes, and who supplied assurance?
- Which controls must remain in place?
- Where did the request move between people without a clear owner?
- What small experiment could improve the next decision safely?

Close by restating the decision boundary, owner and review date. Do not suggest removing mandatory controls or promise shorter approval times. Service Blueprint sellers can use these questions to distinguish one approval problem from a wider handoff problem.

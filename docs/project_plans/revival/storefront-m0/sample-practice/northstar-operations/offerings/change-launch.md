## What it is
A practical readiness and enablement engagement for an already defined operational change.

## What it entails
1. Identify affected teams and the work that will change.
2. Check role readiness through short conversations.
3. Agree messages, support routes and a launch review.

## Examples
A fictional regional service desk used a readiness map to identify teams needing a rehearsal before a new intake process launched. This is an illustrative scenario, not client evidence.

## What it is
A focused review of one recurring decision, its inputs, criteria and accountable owner.

## What it entails
1. Follow one recent decision from request to answer.
2. Separate required controls from accidental waiting.
3. Agree a bounded experiment and a review date.

## Examples
A fictional purchasing team discovered that two reviewers were checking the same criterion. They agreed to test a single named decision owner while preserving mandatory controls. No quantified savings are asserted.

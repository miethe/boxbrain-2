## What it is
A facilitated service-design engagement that makes work across teams visible.

## What it entails
1. Listen to teams: understand requests, constraints and lived experience.
2. Map the handoffs: connect people, work and ownership from request to close.
3. Agree a service blueprint: prioritize a small improvement backlog with owners.

## Examples
A clearer handoff in six weeks: a fictional support team used a shared map to agree who receives a request next. Six weeks describes this invented engagement, not a promised duration or a measured performance gain. See the fictional case study in the collateral library.

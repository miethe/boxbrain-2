## Listen for
Some teams are not ready to launch.

## Ask
Which role needs to do something differently on day one?

## Position
A readiness map, clear support routes and a launch review.

## Avoid promising
Guaranteed adoption or training as a substitute for clear ownership.

## Next step
Use the Change readiness paper to agree a readiness conversation.

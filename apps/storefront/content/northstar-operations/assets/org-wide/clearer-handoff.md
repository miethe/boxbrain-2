# A clearer handoff in six weeks
Fictional case study. No real client or measured results.

## Situation
An invented support team received incomplete requests from several departments. Each team described the boundary differently.

## Approach
Over an illustrative six-week engagement, facilitators listened to the teams, mapped the request path and brought owners together to compare handoffs.

## What changed
The teams agreed a shared map and selected one intake handoff for an experiment. This story claims no measured time saving, revenue gain or customer outcome.

## Transferable lesson
Use a concrete request to expose ownership ambiguity. Validate whether this approach fits the next client's constraints rather than treating this fiction as evidence.

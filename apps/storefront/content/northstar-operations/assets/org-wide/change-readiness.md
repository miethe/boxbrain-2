# Change readiness paper
Original fictional sample white paper.

## Readiness is a work question
A launch message cannot establish that people know how to do changed work. Start by naming the role, the new task and the support needed when it goes wrong.

## Three checks
Ask whether the role understands the change, can rehearse the task and knows where to seek help. Record uncertainty without turning it into a single misleading score.

## Build a small launch plan
Name affected roles, owners of support routes and a time to review the rollout. A rehearsal can reveal unclear handoffs before a wider launch.

## Limits
This is a framing paper, not compliance advice, a survey instrument or evidence of adoption. Adapt it with the teams and required assurance owners.
